# Transhumanism (TRNSHM)

> Status: draft — unreviewed. Filled from the sources listed; LIO axes use the 0–4 scale (P1). The v7.1 scores and note are unchanged.

## Summary

Transhumanism is a 'philosophical and scientific movement that advocates the use of current and emerging technologies … to augment human capabilities and improve the human condition' toward the 'posthuman' [S1]. Julian Huxley popularized the term in 1957 [S1].

## Core metaphysics

Varies: mostly materialist, atheist or agnostic; some religious adaptations [S1].
